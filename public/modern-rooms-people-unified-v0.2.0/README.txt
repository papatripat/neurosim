MODERN ROOMS & PEOPLE — UNIFIED PACK
Version 0.2.0 | HappyHustle

Six selected PixelLab Pro characters, including the completely rebuilt Resident.
48 idle/walk clips, 408 animation frames and 48 standing views.
32 furniture/prop designs, 16 tiles and three furnished room layouts.

START: Open index.html for the pack overview, then examples/browser/index.html
for the moving six-person room and lineup comparison. No installation needed.
GODOT: Import project.godot in Godot 4. WASD/arrows move; Tab selects a person;
R resets. The gold foot marker identifies the selected person.

Art/characters contains original PNGs, atlases, eight-direction standing views,
native timings/pivots, and aligned 64x64 sheets for consistent importing.
Art/props and art/tiles contain named PNGs and grid atlases.
Source/environment contains editable Aseprite props and tiles.
Read IMPORT-GUIDE.txt, ASSET-LIST.txt, manifest.json and LICENSE.txt.

Scope: character motion covers idle and walk in four directions. Standing
turnarounds have eight directions. Occupation actions are not included.
The room samples are visual/controller examples. Furniture is decorative;
there are no prop collisions, navigation, sitting or interactive work actions.
Tiles form a small construction kit rather than a complete autotile system.
