MODERN ROOMS & PEOPLE — IMPORT GUIDE

Choose one character representation:
1. Native: native.json stores exact frame paths, dimensions, foot pivots,
timings and source sheets. Draw at ground position minus the clip pivot.
2. Aligned: aligned.png and aligned.json use 64x64 cells and pivot (32,54).
Every cell preserves native sprite pixels; only transparent padding changes.
Use the frame rectangles in JSON. Blank end-of-row cells are not extra frames.
3. Godot: godot-resources has SpriteFrames and reusable Node2D scenes.
The child AnimatedSprite2D uses position (-32,-54) and centered=false.
Match the chosen direction to idle_south/east/north/west or walk equivalents.
Use native.json transitions.walk_entry_frame for a close idle-to-walk handoff.

Character standing PNGs: 48x48. Body heights are 36–38px in the south view.
Native idle canvases vary by character; walk canvases are 64x64.
There are nine idle timeline frames and eight walk frames per direction.
Idle frames last 300ms each; walk frames last 100ms each.
Eight standing directions do not imply eight-direction animated motion.

Tiles: 32x32, zero margin/spacing, eight columns in tiles.png.
Props: 64x64 cells, pivot (32,58), eight columns in props.png.
Do not stretch each prop to fill its cell. Sort actors/props by ground Y;
wall decorations and rugs draw behind actors. manifest.json lists placements.
Rooms-ground.png contains walls and floors. Rooms-composed.png is a static
example using the new Resident and current cast; use individual assets in games.
Use nearest-neighbor filtering, whole-pixel positions and integer display zoom.

Environment art uses the included 24-color palette. Character palettes remain
as generated and are not restricted to the environment palette.
The environment Aseprite files keep their original layers. Character artwork
is supplied as editable RGBA PNGs and frame sheets, without separate body layers.
